## App name (max 30)

```
OurDIU
```

The Play app is "OurDIU" (package `com.ourdiu.app`), and so is the launcher
label. Inside, each product is a space with its own name: the Question Bank is
live, the Class Routine and the Marketplace say "Coming soon". Only describe what
the app does today; mention products that aren't out yet as coming soon, never
as features. Keep the disclaimer at the end of the description: with "DIU" in
the name, Play's impersonation policy expects it to be clear the app isn't the
university's own.

## Full description (max 4000)

```
OurDIU is a free app for students of Daffodil International University (DIU). It starts with the Question Bank: a library of past exam question papers, shared by students. Find the paper you need in seconds, read it on your phone, and give back by sharing the ones you have.

A class routine and a student marketplace are coming soon, in the same app with the same account.

FIND THE RIGHT PAPER
• Browse by department and course, or search by course name or code.
• Filter by semester and exam type: midterm, final, quiz and more.
• See what's new and what's popular with other students.

READ AND SAVE
• A fast, full-screen reader built for question papers.
• Save papers to come back to before your exam.
• Share a paper with classmates in one tap.

SHARE A PAPER
• Scan a paper with your camera, pick a PDF from your files, or share one to OurDIU from WhatsApp, Messenger or Drive.
• The app suggests the course, semester and exam type for you.
• Watch your paper get checked and published, and see how many students it has helped.

SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE
Browsing and reading need no account. Sign in with Google to like the useful papers and report problems, and with your DIU account to share papers. One account works across OurDIU.

FREE, WITHOUT ADS
No ads, no paywalls, no premium papers. OurDIU is built and looked after by a DIU student.

OurDIU is an independent project, not an official app of Daffodil International University. Papers are shared by students; if you find a problem with one, report it from the paper's menu.

Website: https://ourdiu.com
```
