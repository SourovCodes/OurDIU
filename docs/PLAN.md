# OurDIU – project plan

The plan and the decisions behind it, kept in the repository so anyone (or any Claude session) picking the project up knows what exists, what comes next and why. Update it when a decision changes or a step is done. `CLAUDE.md` has the day-to-day working rules; `README.md` the architecture and setup.

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

## What OurDIU is

[ourdiu.com](https://ourdiu.com) is one home for Daffodil International University (DIU) student tools, on the web and in one mobile app:

| Product       | Path         | Status                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Question Bank | `/questions` | Live at [diuqbank.com](https://diuqbank.com) (repo `../QuestionBank`); moved here on the `question-bank` branch, waiting for the cutover |
| Class Routine | `/routine`   | Students' routines built (decisions 29–32); "coming soon" until a version is live on production                                          |
| Marketplace   | `/market`    | "Coming soon" placeholder; later                                                                                                         |

Most users come from the question bank, so it moves in first and the others are discovered from it.

## Decisions (and why)

1. **One repository, one Cloudflare Worker, one origin, a path per product.** Pages at `/<product>`, product API at `/api/v1/<product>/…`. Session cookies stay first-party, no CORS, one API for the app. Subdomains or separate Workers were rejected: they'd need cross-domain auth for no gain at this size.
2. **One account system for everything.** Better Auth, Google sign-in only. Any Google account can sign in (decided 1 October 2026; before that, new accounts needed a DIU address). Contributing question papers needs a DIU address (`@diu.edu.bd`, `@s.diu.edu.bd`) or the admin role (`canContribute` in `@ourdiu/shared`; `requireContributor` on the API). Addresses in the `ADMIN_EMAILS` secret are made admins when they sign up.
3. **The products are not interconnected.** They share accounts, departments and the look, nothing else: no product shows another's data. Each has its own tables (prefixed with its name, except the question bank's, which keep their existing names, see below), its own services and API namespace, its own admin section.
4. **Presentation: "option C", separate spaces.** `ourdiu.com` only asks "What do you need?" (and offers to continue where you left off). Each product is a space with its own header/menu, colour and (in the app) its own bottom tabs. A switcher next to the product name moves between spaces. Question bank users arriving from diuqbank.com land straight in their space; a dismissible banner (web) or one-time hint (app) tells them about the routine.
5. **Mobile app: one Flutter app, Material 3 (Expressive), on Android and iOS.** It's the existing OurDIU app (`com.ourdiu.app`, today in `../QuestionBank/apps/mobile`), extended with the spaces and the switcher. Its design system (indigo `#4F39F6`, Roboto Flex `expressive()` headings, the exam-type shapes) stays. iOS gets Material too, with only platform behaviours (back swipe, scrolling physics, dialogs) adapted. A full Cupertino design was mocked up and rejected: twice the design work for a mostly-Android audience.
6. **Space colours**: Question Bank indigo `#4F39F6`, Class Routine teal (`#006B5B` / container `#B9F0E3`), Marketplace rose.
7. **The question bank keeps its API paths** (`/api/v1/questions`, `/submissions`, `/contributors`, `/taxonomy`, `/me`, …) instead of moving under `/api/v1/questions/…`: the published app calls them, and installed copies keep calling them for months. Its tables keep their names (`questions`, `submissions`, `courses`, …) so the production database can be adopted as is. New products follow the prefix rules.
8. **The routine is rebuilt from scratch** (not ported from `../better-routine-scrapper`), CSE only at first, from the university's PDF (e.g. [CSE routine v3.1](https://webbackend.daffodilvarsity.edu.bd/noticeFile/cse-class-routine-v31-f4f0af7da9.pdf)). Admins upload the PDF and OurDIU reads it (decisions 30–31; at first it was turned into a JSON file outside OurDIU, decision 29).

Design mockups (website, Material app, and the rejected iOS style): <https://claude.ai/artifact/RfDHeYL7wiGdBDo2QqKokQ>. The website redesign that brings it up to the app (approved 2 October 2026): <https://claude.ai/artifact/Mh2DpngKUFRop8H1UQ7Vnj>.

9. **The website follows the app's design** (decided 2 October 2026). Same palette (indigo with tinted greys, the exam-type colours), Roboto Flex `font-expressive` headings over Inter, the exam badges (`ExamBadge`, shapes from `apps/mobile/lib/theme/exam_shape.dart`), search first, and the app's department → course → semester path. Pages: `/questions/departments/:id` (courses A–Z) and `/questions/courses/:id` (exams by semester, exam-type filter, "same course, other names").
10. **Platform pages keep the visitor's space** (2 October 2026). Account, about, contact, legal, login and missing pages show the header of the space the visitor was last in (the `ourdiu_space` cookie, `useSpace`); "Back to …" links and logging out lead to that space's home, not the hub. The hub itself belongs to no space.
11. **Two page widths** (2 October 2026): browsing pages (lists, papers, courses, departments) use the full container; single-purpose pages (account, about, contact, legal, login, `/app`) share `NARROW_PAGE` (`max-w-4xl`, centred).
12. **Android releases go straight to testers** (2 October 2026): a `mobile-v*` tag uploads to the closed testing track (`alpha`) as a completed release, so testers get it from the Play Store once Google approves it; the tag's message becomes "What's new". `PLAY_TRACK` / `PLAY_RELEASE_STATUS` repository variables override this.
13. **ourdiu.com links open in the app** (Android App Links, 2 October 2026): only `https://ourdiu.com/questions…` (not `www`, which redirects and so can't be verified). The site serves `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` with the fingerprints Play Console generates for the app (the app signing key first; not the upload key); `webLinkLocation` in `apps/mobile/lib/router.dart` maps the site's paths to the app's screens.
14. **Public PDFs are stamped as well as credited** (2 October 2026): the watermark job uses the PDF processor's `credit-watermark-compress` endpoint: the credit line on top of every page as before, plus the site's domain (`ourdiu.com`) stamped faintly at random positions and angles. Admins remake every copy with **Redo all watermarks** (`POST /api/v1/admin/submissions/watermark/all`).
15. **Everything clickable shows the hand cursor and a visible hover** (2 October 2026). Tailwind 4 gives buttons the arrow, so a base rule in `app.css` gives the hand to enabled buttons, menu items, options, tabs and similar controls (cmdk's `data-disabled="false"` counts as enabled). Hover, focus and open states use Material's state layer, the text colour at 8% (`--state-layer`) laid over the element's own surface with the `state-layer` utility (`hover:state-layer`, `focus:state-layer`, `data-[highlighted]:state-layer`, …), instead of swapping to a fixed colour: those matched the surface they sat on (menus were invisible in dark mode, where `popover` equals `accent`; cards changed by a contrast of 1.06 in light mode). The tint is a plain token, not `color-mix(currentColor …)`, whose compiled fallback for older browsers would paint the element solid. Filled buttons keep their own hover; outline buttons hover at 12% primary.
16. **e2e tests run beside `pnpm dev`** (2 October 2026): on port 5174 (`e2e/env.ts`) with their own local D1 and R2 in `.wrangler/e2e-state`, which `e2e/prepare-state.mjs` wipes, migrates and seeds before every run. They no longer need `pnpm dev` stopped or the dev database seeded, and no longer leave test records in it.
17. **Reviews are a conversation** (2 October 2026). Besides publishing and rejecting, an admin can **ask for changes** (status `changes_requested`, shown as "Needs changes"): the paper goes back to its uploader, who can edit its details, replace its file and reply, then **resubmits** it to the review queue. Every message and step (requested, edited, file replaced, resubmitted, published, rejected, back to review) is kept in `submission_messages` with the paper, and removed with it. Uploaders see admins as "Reviewer". Rejecting stays the final "no" (it now suggests asking for changes instead). A paper that ever had changes asked for is never auto-published by the AI check: the admin asked to see it again. Counters: `questions.pending_review_count` counts `changes_requested` too (migration 0011); `submissions.uploader_unread` / `admin_unread` drive the "new messages" badges. Notices are on the site only for now (header dot and "N papers need you", the list's banner and badges); email can come later.
18. **Search: keep "DIU Question Bank"** (2 October 2026). diuqbank.com's last 16 months in Search Console: 142K clicks, ~72% from the one query "diu question bank" (102K clicks, position 1), then "question bank diu", "diu question bank cse" / "swe", "diu qbank", "diuqbank"; ~95% of clicks landed on the home page. "OurDIU" is one word to Google, so it doesn't match "diu": the question bank's titles, headings and text say **DIU Question Bank** (`QB_NAME` in `app/lib/seo.ts`) and the Question Bank home says "formerly DIU QBank (diuqbank.com)" for searches of the old name, department pages are titled "DIU CSE Question Bank", course and paper pages lead with the course name ("… previous questions", "… Final Question, Summer 25"). Every page gets a canonical URL (filters and tracking dropped, `?page=` kept), Open Graph tags and `public/og.png` (drawn by `apps/web/tool/og-image.mjs`) for links shared in Facebook groups; the hub has WebSite JSON-LD (alternate names "DIU Question Bank", "DIU QBank", "diuqbank"), department, course and paper pages BreadcrumbList. The sitemap lists course pages too. Routes set titles through `pageMeta`.
19. **The move is told, for search and AI answers** (2 October 2026). `/diuqbank` says what became of diuqbank.com: when, where each page is now, why OurDIU, what stays the same, and an FAQ, written as plain facts that AI overviews and assistants can quote, with FAQPage JSON-LD. It sits at the root, not under `/questions`, because the Android app opens `/questions/*` links (decision 13); `productAt` still puts it in the question bank's space (`PRODUCT_PAGES`). The Question Bank home ("formerly diuqbank.com") and /about link to it. `/llms.txt` (llmstxt.org) describes the site in Markdown with live numbers, departments, the top courses and the same FAQ; all of it comes from `app/lib/move.ts`. The hub's WebSite JSON-LD names its publisher (`AUTHOR`). AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot) are not blocked; keep it that way in Cloudflare (AI Crawl Control) and robots.txt.
20. **Admins merge duplicates** (3 October 2026). Uploaders and the AI file papers under alternative spellings, so the catalog (`/admin/questions/catalog`) lets an admin select entries of one kind (departments, courses, semesters or exam types), pick the one to keep, see a dry-run preview of what moves, and merge: everything filed under the others moves to it and they are deleted (`POST /api/v1/admin/{kind}/merge`, `services/merge.ts`, one D1 batch with `PRAGMA defer_foreign_keys` for the composite course/department keys). Questions that become the same exam are combined into one (papers, saves and views move to it). Courses merge only within a department; merging departments combines their courses with the same name (`catalogKey`) and moves the rest. Like the earlier diuqbank's merge (QuestionBank repo, `lib/merge.ts`). Removed ids are remembered in `merged_ids` (migration 0012, chains kept flat), and `GET /api/v1/merged/{kind}/{id}` lets old question, course and department pages 301 to where they went, so search rankings and saved links survive. The courses tab has a "Likely duplicates" filter (same name up to a plural or case, in one department).
21. **Profile images are served from the files domain** (3 October 2026). They are public anyway, so `user.image` holds `FILES_URL/avatars/{id}` (files.ourdiu.com, the R2 bucket's public domain, with an immutable `Cache-Control` set on the object) instead of a URL that streams them through the Worker. Images set before keep `/api/v1/avatars/{id}`, which now 301s there (and still serves from R2 locally, where `FILES_URL` is empty). New uploads and Google photos copied at sign-up get the new URL. Better Auth's `update-user` refuses `image` (400): images change only through `/api/v1/me/avatar`, so nobody can show an arbitrary URL or point at another user's stored image (which replacing theirs would have deleted).

22. **Papers' text is read and searchable** (3 October 2026). The AI reads each paper's full text (header, questions, marks; maths in plain text, tables with " | ", figures as `[Figure: …]`, handwriting left out) in the same Gemini call as the upload check (`TEXT_INSTRUCTIONS` in `services/paper-text.ts`), stored in `submission_texts` (migration 0013), one row per paper, with `error` when a read failed. Older published papers are read by a backfill: **Read missing texts** on the admin submissions page (`POST /api/v1/admin/submissions/text`) queues them on the analysis queue as `{ kind: "text" }` jobs, one AI call each (no new queue or binding). Only published papers' text is served: `text` on each submission of `GET /api/v1/questions/{id}`, folded under the PDF on the paper page ("Read the questions as text", in the HTML for search engines), and searched by `GET /api/v1/questions/search` through an FTS5 index (`submission_texts_fts`, migration 0014, triggers keep it in step; porter stemming, so "sorting" finds "sort"; every word must match, the last may be a prefix; one hit per exam, its best copy, with a snippet). The website's `/questions/search` shows matching courses first, then the papers; the course search dialog offers "Find … in papers' text". The app has the generated client but no search screen yet. Note: `wrangler d1 export` refuses databases with virtual tables (drop `submission_texts_fts`, export, recreate it from migration 0014 and `rebuild`).
23. **An admission guide for applicants** (3 October 2026). Search Console showed ~2K impressions for "daffodil university admission test question" and similar at positions 2–5 with no page for them. `/admission` (at the root like `/diuqbank`, in the question bank's space) is a static guide: how to apply, who can apply, the test (one hour, times by faculty), semesters, documents, waivers, an FAQ with FAQPage JSON-LD and the admission office's contacts. Its facts come from DIU's admission portal FAQ and DIU's FAQ, checked on 3 October 2026 (`lib/admission.ts`, with the sources listed on the page); it says plainly that OurDIU isn't DIU and that rules change, and asks applicants to send admission test papers. Linked from the footer and in the sitemap.
24. **"Most viewed today" instead of all-time** (3 October 2026). The all-time `view_count` kept the same old papers on top forever, so the Question Bank home and the app's carousel now rank by the last 24 hours. Every counted question page view (still once per browser a day) also adds to its hour's row in `question_view_hours` (migration 0015), in the same D1 batch as the all-time counter. A Cron Trigger every 10 minutes (`triggers.crons` in `wrangler.jsonc`, the API's `scheduled` handler, `services/trending.ts`) drops hours past the window and rebuilds `trending_questions` (the top 100 exams with their views) and `trending_courses` (the top 20 courses, a course's views being its exams'), in one batch, so lists read a few indexed rows instead of summing the window on every home load. `sort=trending` lists them (`sort=popular` stays all-time for installed apps); every list's `viewsToday` gives a question's views if it is in the top 100. The home shows "Most viewed today" with "N views today" when four courses were viewed enough to fill the row, and otherwise the all-time "Most viewed": the two are never mixed. Below it, "Most viewed courses today" (`GET /api/v1/courses/trending`) ranks up to six courses, shown only when at least four were viewed (no all-time stand-in). Merges add a combined exam's hours to the kept one. Workers Analytics Engine was considered and rejected at this size (a few thousand counted views a day): reading it needs its SQL API over HTTPS with an account API token as a secret, it samples at high volume, it can't be queried locally (so the cron couldn't be tested), and the cron would still have to write the result to D1 to join it with courses and papers. Worth revisiting at millions of views a day or for richer analytics (country, referrer). KV (eventually consistent, paid writes) was rejected too.
25. **No product chooser until a second product is live** (3 October 2026). With only the Question Bank live, the hub ("What do you need?") and the app's first-launch chooser were a step in the way. The website's `HUB_LIVE` (`app/lib/products.ts`) is false: `/` answers with a temporary 302 to `/questions` (browsers don't cache it, so the hub can return), keeping the visitor's query; the "All of OurDIU" links are hidden, `/` is out of the sitemap, and the WebSite JSON-LD (site name, alternate names) is on `/questions`. The app's `showChooser` (`lib/spaces/space.dart`) is false: it opens in the Question Bank, also for someone who last looked at a coming-soon product. The product switchers stay, so the coming-soon pages can still be found. Turn both on when the Class Routine or the Marketplace launches.

26. **The app keeps itself up to date** (3 October 2026; mockups approved: <https://claude.ai/artifact/CJCZ3ZAzNDrLZEUcVj9G9E>). Play's auto-update misses people who turned it off, and testers often wait days. At launch, after the first frame, the app (`lib/data/app_update.dart`, `features/update/update_gate.dart`) first asks the API for the oldest version that still works (`GET /api/v1/app/android`, the `ANDROID_MIN_VERSION` var in `wrangler.jsonc`, 1.6.0 to start): an older app shows only "Time to update OurDIU" with "Update on Google Play". Raise it only for a breaking change or a serious bug, since `/api/v1` stays backward compatible. Otherwise Google Play's in-app update (`in_app_update`, flexible): Play shows its own "Update available" sheet, downloads in the background, and the app's snackbar offers **Restart** (unrestarted, Play installs it when the app closes). "No thanks" isn't asked again for that version for 3 days. Only release builds installed from Play can check, so it's tested with fakes and on a Play build. Offline or failing, nothing blocks.
27. **Tuned from production data** (3 October 2026). After the first days live, D1 insights showed the database isn't slow (0.5 ms a query on average) but Workers analytics showed `/__manifest` as the Worker's busiest path (11.6k requests a day, more than all question pages): React Router's lazy route discovery asking which routes the links on a page lead to, a round trip before many clicks. With about 45 routes the whole manifest is 3.6 KB gzipped, so `routeDiscovery: { mode: "initial" }` (`react-router.config.ts`) ships it with the page. D1 also read about 600 rows for each course + exam type list or count (24k a week, 39% of rows read): without `ANALYZE` stats SQLite chose `questions_exam_type_id_idx`, so migration 0016 adds `questions_course_id_exam_type_id_idx`. Not worth it at this size: edge-caching API or page responses (D1 time is negligible and the app's API traffic is small). Left to the owner: a Cache Everything rule for `files.ourdiu.com` (avatars have no extension, so they aren't cached at the edge) and moving old `/api/v1/avatars/…` image URLs to the files domain.
28. **Missing paper texts are read a little at a time** (3 October 2026). Queueing all ~2,650 published papers at once (**Read missing texts**, decision 22) would blow through Gemini's free tier, which isn't published per model but is reported at about 20 requests a day for Gemini 3.8 Flash and about 500 for Flash-Lite (check AI Studio's rate-limit page; daily quotas reset at midnight Pacific). So text-only reads use their own model, `GEMINI_TEXT_MODEL` (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, in `wrangler.jsonc`), keeping 3.8 Flash's small quota for the upload check, and a second cron (`TEXT_CRON`, every 5 minutes, `src/index.ts`) reads one paper per run (`readNextMissingText` in `services/paper-text.ts`): 288 calls a day at most, about nine days for the backlog. Never-read papers go first, oldest first; a failed read is stored straight away and retried after a week, except Gemini's 429, which leaves the paper for the next run. Without `GEMINI_API_KEY` (local, e2e) the cron does nothing. To go faster or slower, change the cron; the admin button still queues everything at once (only worth it on a paid key).

29. **The Class Routine starts with students' routines, from uploaded JSON files** (4 October 2026; mockups approved: <https://claude.ai/artifact/XosQGCBUFB5ewdRhyrBjSa>). DIU publishes each department's routine as a PDF and revises it during the semester. OurDIU doesn't read the PDF: it's turned into a JSON file outside OurDIU (by hand or with an AI chat) and an admin uploads that. The format (`routineFileSchema`, `packages/shared/src/schemas/routine.ts`) is one file per version: department, version as printed ("4.1"), slots, optional course titles and teachers' names, and every class (day `SAT`–`FRI`, 24-hour `start`/`end` on slot edges, course code, section, optional `labGroup` such as `B1` for 67_B1, room, `roomType: "lab"`, teacher initials). `/admin/routine/format` explains it and offers an example, the JSON schema (generated from the Zod schema) and **Copy AI instructions** to paste into a chat with the PDF. Uploads are checked strictly: a file that breaks a rule is refused with each problem's place (`classes[412].start`); possible slips that DIU's routine may really have (a section, room or teacher in two places at once, a class listed twice, a course without a title) are warnings on the draft, not refusals. Each version is a draft until an admin makes it live; one version per department is live (a partial unique index), the live one becomes "previous" in the same batch and can be made live again (rollback). Any version but the live one can be deleted, with its uploaded file. The review page previews any section of the version as students will see it (`GET /api/v1/admin/routine/versions/{id}/sections/{section}`). Retake sections (anything not like `67_B`, e.g. `RE_A(3C)`) gather several courses at the same times by design: they aren't warned about as clashes, their page explains them instead of showing a "next class", and the week stacks classes at the same time in lanes. Courses without a title are one warning listing them. The review compares a draft with the live version (classes moved, room or teacher changes, added/removed classes and sections). Course titles and teachers' names are kept across versions (`routine_courses`, `routine_teachers`, written when a version goes live), so a file can leave them out. CSE only for now; tables and URLs carry the department (`/routine/cse/67_B`, `/api/v1/routine/cse/…`) so others can follow. Students get, in the question bank's shapes (hero and big search, tonal tiles, filter chips, a side panel): `/routine` (search "67b1" finds lab group B1 of 67_B; every section by batch; "My section" in the `ourdiu_routine` cookie, whose day leads the page: the class you're in, the next one, or tomorrow's first), `/routine/cse/{section}?group=B1` (a Today card in Dhaka time, the week as the printed grid on wide screens and dated day tabs on phones, courses, a link to DIU's PDF when the file names it; a section written another way, like `67-b1`, redirects to it, and a missing one offers the search and look-alikes), and a PDF of the week (`GET /api/v1/routine/{department}/sections/{section}/pdf?group=`): one A4 page made on request with `pdf-lib`, OurDIU-branded (no DIU logo: it isn't an official document), with the version, the lab group and a QR code to the live page. It's made on request rather than stored in R2 per version: it takes milliseconds and changes with the version anyway. Until a version is live, `/routine` stays "coming soon"; the product's `status`, the hub (`HUB_LIVE`) and the app's chooser change only at launch (decision 25).
30. **EEE's routine PDF is read by OurDIU; JSON files stay the format** (4 October 2026). Turning a PDF into a file by hand or with an AI chat for every version is work, and an AI can misread a room or a time without saying so. So for a department whose PDF layout OurDIU knows, an admin uploads DIU's PDF as it is (`POST /api/v1/admin/routine/versions/pdf`) and a reader for that layout (`apps/api/src/services/routine/import/`, with `unpdf` for the text and where it sits on the page) turns it into the same routine file, which then goes through the same checks, warnings, review and make-live as an uploaded file. The PDF is kept with the version (`pdf_key`, downloadable from the review), and so is the file read from it, so a misread can be fixed by hand and uploaded as a file. Only EEE's layout is known so far (fall 2026, version 4.0): a page per day, each a grid of rooms by one-hour slots (classrooms, then labs under a second header), cells of level-term and section ("1-2 B"), course and section with an optional lab group ("0713-121 B1") and teacher's initials, then the teachers' list (names are read; phone numbers and emails aren't). The reader merges a lab's back-to-back cells into one class, splits a cell two sections share, takes room names over several lines, and never guesses silently: what it couldn't read, or read with a guess (the section's letter disagreeing with the course's, a lab's second cell without its group), is listed first on the draft as `unreadable` warnings. A PDF of an unknown layout is refused (`UNKNOWN_ROUTINE_PDF`); CSE stays a JSON file until a reader for its master PDF is written. Not an AI reader: deterministic, exact and free. EEE sections are written as printed ("1-2 B", lab group "1-2 B1"); page addresses put an underscore for the space (`/routine/eee/1-2_B`) and the API finds either. Course titles aren't in EEE's PDF, so its students see codes (0713-121) until titles are added. Course titles and teachers' names are per department (migration 0018): EEE's "SD" isn't CSE's. The routine home is per department (`/routine/cse`, `/routine/eee`, a switch between them; `/routine` opens the department of "My section", else the first with a routine). The app is CSE only for now.
31. **Routines come only from DIU's PDFs; admins keep course titles and teachers' details** (4 October 2026, replacing the JSON uploads of decisions 29–30). CSE's PDF has its reader too (`import/cse.ts`, CSE v4.1: the days one after another, a day starting anywhere on a page and running on over the next; per 90-minute slot a cell of room, course with its section ("CSE322(67_J1)" is 67_J, lab group J1) and teacher, the room again in every cell and "(COM LAB)" or a "…Lab)" line under a lab's; a course too long for its cell wraps onto the next line; placeholder teachers like "NT-1" and "EEE_1"). Retake sections (`RE_A(3C)`, `RE_A1(1.5C)`, `63_RE_B`) are left out: OurDIU shows batches' own sections. Slips in DIU's section names are tidied and noted once each (a missing bracket is added). Checked against the real v4.1: every one of its 1,961 course cells read; without the retakes, 1,450 classes in 163 sections (batches 63–73), no notes, no clashes. So the JSON format, its upload, its download, the format page and the AI instructions are gone: `POST /api/v1/admin/routine/versions` takes the PDF, which is the version's file (migration 0019 drops `pdf_key` and the versions' `courses`). The PDFs have no course titles (and CSE's no teachers' names), so `/admin/routine/courses` and `/admin/routine/teachers` (per department, listing every code and initials in the department's versions and any added by hand) let admins add titles and teachers' names, the room where they sit, email and phone (`PUT/DELETE /api/v1/admin/routine/courses/{department}/{code}`, `…/teachers/{department}/{initials}`); students see them right away, in every version. A PDF that lists teachers (EEE's, with phones and emails) adds them when its version goes live, filling in only what's empty, so admins' edits stay. Students see a section's teachers with their room, email and phone on its page; the privacy policy says so. Tests draw routines in each layout (`apps/api/test/cse-routine-pdf.ts`, `eee-routine-pdf.ts`), and the seed's routine is such a PDF (`seeds/routine-cse-4.1.pdf`).
32. **Any version can be deleted, and renumbered** (4 October 2026). Deleting the live version is allowed: the department has no routine ("coming soon") until another version is made live, and the confirmation says so. A version's number is the one printed on the PDF unless the admin types another at upload (for a PDF without one, or a wrong one), and can be changed later (`PATCH /api/v1/admin/routine/versions/{id}`; a department's numbers are each used once). Titles and names are typed in the lists' rows (Enter saves and goes on to the next row, clearing a title removes it; a teacher's room, email and phone are in a dialog), and each teacher's courses are shown to tell who "MSA" is; counts are in the current version (the live one, else the newest). A version's review counts its titled courses and named teachers when shown (no longer a stored "untitled" warning, which went stale), with links to the ones missing. Students see a teacher only once there's more than initials to show, in cards under the week. The course titles' and teachers' lists are paged (25 a page, searched and filtered through the URL like the users' list) and take bulk removal: select rows, or every row on a page, and remove their titles or teachers' details at once (`POST /api/v1/admin/routine/courses/remove`, `…/teachers/remove`). The app shows a section's teachers under its courses, and tapping a teacher or a class opens a sheet with the room where the teacher sits, their email and phone (which open the mail or phone app; a long press copies).
